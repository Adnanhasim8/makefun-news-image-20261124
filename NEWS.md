## Lead story

1. **COP31 climate talks collapse without a deal — Antalya, Türkiye.**
   After the longest COP in history (talks ran ~50 hours into overtime),
   more than 90 nations rejected the final "Antalya Pact" text because it
   never mentioned fossil fuels; the EU and small-island states refused to
   endorse it, while Saudi Arabia, Russia, China and India opposed any
   fossil-fuel wording. First COP since Copenhagen 2009 to end with no
   overarching agreement. The collapse itself was on Sunday 22 Nov; the
   fallout, blame game and analysis dominated 24 Nov coverage.
   Sources: CNN (23 Nov, updated 24 Nov), Financial Times, Grist,
   Democracy Now! (24 Nov), The Daily Edition No. 279 (24 Nov).

## The other stories

2. **Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya — Benghazi.** President
   Zelenskyy confirmed on 24 Nov that Ukrainian sea drones sank the
   Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval base (seven drones, ~2,000 km
   from Ukraine) and hit the cargo ship Angara; the UN called for a probe
   and Libya's eastern government complained to the UN.
   Sources: NPR, The Guardian, CBS News, ABC News, Middle East Eye (23–24 Nov).
3. **Russia keeps testing NATO's skies — Europe.** The Conversation counts
   25 Russian airspace violations against NATO members in 2026 (4x the 2024
   total); a Russian drone briefly entered Polish airspace on 24 Nov, days
   after missiles and a drone crashed in Poland on 20 Nov.
   Sources: The Conversation, Polish Armed Forces via Fiat Elpis live desk
   (24 Nov), The Daily Edition (24 Nov).
4. **US–Iran standoff sharpens at Hormuz — Middle East.** The UK logged
   record Iranian attacks in the Strait of Hormuz; US VP Vance and Secretary
   of State Rubio met Qatar's PM about Iran negotiations; President Trump
   said he is inclined to resume strikes on Iran after the New Year if talks
   fail. Sources: IQMIA Worldwide News Summary 2026-11-24, Washington Post
   via Fiat Elpis (24 Nov), The Daily Edition (24 Nov).
5. **Israeli strikes kill three in Gaza — Middle East.** Al Jazeera: at
   least three killed, including a UNRWA worker. Oxfam/CEOBS: contamination
   risks across more than 99% of Gaza from the military assault.
   Sources: Al Jazeera via IQMIA (24 Nov); Oxfam/CEOBS via 23 Nov round-up.
6. **Factory fire kills 24 in Indonesia — Banten.** A candy factory fire in
   Cikande killed at least 24 workers, with about 30–36 still missing;
   rescuers turned to DNA identification. Sources: The Guardian, ABC News,
   Sky News (24 Nov).
7. **US blacklists Megaspeed in Nvidia spy case — Asia.** The US Bureau of
   Industry and Security put Singapore-based Megaspeed International and six
   other Southeast Asian firms on the Entity List over advanced US tech and
   an alleged Nvidia spy scandal. Source: Daily Summary Of World, 24 Nov.
8. **Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang — Business.** Nvidia's board
   unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after his ouster, following
   intervention by President Trump; the FT reported Trump made up to $15m on
   Nvidia shares after Huang's return. Sources: AP, FT, Mother Jones (24 Nov).
9. **Democrats win 258 House seats — USA.** Democrats won at least 258
   seats, their largest House majority since 1992; Melat Kiros, 29, became
   the first Gen Z woman elected to Congress. Sources: Le Monde, SVPN news
   archive 24 Nov, 23 Nov round-up.
10. **States sue SpaceX over Grok images — USA.** Oregon and Virginia sued

    Source: IQMIA Worldwide News Summary 2026-11-24.
11. **Car bomb rocks Colombian city — Villavicencio.** A car bomb destroyed

12. **Alcaraz wins first ATP Finals title — Sport.** Carlos Alcaraz beat
